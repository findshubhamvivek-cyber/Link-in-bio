Frequently asked questions

What is Linklease?

Linklease turns your link in bio into a beautiful mini website. Add links, photos, videos, forms, FAQs, testimonials, and your latest Instagram posts, organize them into tabs and folders, and share it all from one link like linklease.co/yourname, or your own domain on Pro.

How long does it take to set up?

About a minute to get started. Enter your Instagram handle and Linklease pulls in your photo, bio, and recent posts to build your first page. You can also start from your website or from scratch. Then make it yours with themes, fonts, and blocks, and go live whenever you're ready.

Can I switch from Linktree?

Yes, and you don't have to start over. Paste your Linktree URL when you sign up and Linklease brings over your links, social icons, photo, and bio. Beacons and most other link pages work too. Look it over, tweak anything you like, then swap the link in your bio when you go live.

Why isn't there a free plan?

Because you're the customer, not the product. Free tiers get paid for somehow, usually with ads, data, or constant upsells, and we'd rather keep your page clean and work for you. You can still build your entire page for free and take as long as you like. When you're ready to go live, every plan starts with a 14-day free trial.

How much does Linklease cost?

Linklease offers two plans: Starter at $5/mo with all the essentials, and Pro at $12/mo which adds custom domains and multi-profile management. Both plans include a 14-day free trial.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes! With the Pro plan, you can connect a custom domain so your page lives at your own URL instead of linklease.co.

Can I manage multiple profiles?

Yes. The Pro plan lets you manage multiple profiles from a single account, each with its own page. Perfect for agencies, teams, or anyone running more than one brand.

What kind of content can I add?

Links, images, videos, music embeds, contact forms, text blocks, folders, social icons, and more. Linklease supports a wide variety of block types so you can showcase your work however you want.

Do I get analytics?

Yes. Linklease includes built-in analytics so you can track page views, link clicks, and see where your audience is coming from.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no contracts or commitments. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings.
